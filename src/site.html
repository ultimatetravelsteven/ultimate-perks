FAQ

Questions venues ask

Who sees my offer?

Working holiday makers, backpackers and touring travellers who have signed in to the Traveller Perks app with an Apple, Google or Facebook account or a tour booking. The app shows offers by town, so you're in front of people who are actually nearby.

How do staff know a perk is real?

The traveller's screen shows a code that changes every 15 seconds with a live clock, so screenshots don't work. Staff can check it by eye, or type or scan it in your portal.

Can I change my offer?

Any time, from your portal. Changes show in the app straight away. We email you on the 1st of each month to check your deals are still right.

What happens after 12 months?

If you've saved a card and left auto-renew on, it renews for another year and you get a tax invoice. We email you 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before, so there are no surprises.

How do payments work?

Payments run through eWay, an Australian payment gateway. Your card details are held by eWay, not by us. We only keep a token so renewals and boosts are one click.

We have more than one location. How does that work?

List your first location for $299, then tap "I'm in multiple locations" in your portal and add the rest for $99 each per year. Travellers get a brand page with every location on a map, and you can roll an offer out to all of them in one click.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. and we'll come back to you, usually the same business day.

Do you take a cut of sales?

No. You pay the listing fee and any boosts you choose. What travellers spend at your venue is yours.
