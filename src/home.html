Questions

Good to know

Who can use Perks?

Any traveller. Backpackers, working holiday makers, people on a Group Tour, people travelling on their own. You don't need to have booked anything with ULTIMATE.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app and every perk are free for travellers.

How do I use a perk?

Open the venue in the app, tap Use and show the live voucher to staff. It changes every few seconds so staff know it's real.

How often can I use a perk?

Once per person per day at each venue, unless the venue says otherwise on the perk.

Where does it work?

The East Coast of Australia from Melbourne to Cairns, including Sydney, Byron Bay, Surfers Paradise, Brisbane, Noosa, Rainbow Beach, Airlie Beach, Magnetic Island and Mission Beach.

I run a venue. How do I get on Perks?

See the venue page to list your bar, restaurant or activity.
